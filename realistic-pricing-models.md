## 🚀 Recommended: Start with $9-19 Pricing

### Why This Works:
1. **Lower barrier to entry** - Easier to get first customers
2. **Builds trust** - Prove value before raising prices
3. **Word of mouth** - More likely to recommend affordable tool
4. **Easier marketing** - "$9/month" vs "$49/month" conversion rates
5. **Gradual increase** - Can raise prices after 6-12 months

## 🎯 Marketing Strategy for Lower Pricing

### Launch Tactics:
1. **"Launch Special"** - First 50 customers get 50% off ($4.50/month)
2. **Beta Testers** - Free lifetime for first 10 users
3. **Referral Program** - $10 credit for each referral
4. **Content Marketing** - Blog posts about social media tips
5. **Free Tools** - Offer free scheduling tools to attract users

### Conversion Optimization:
- **Free trial** - 14 days full access
- **Money-back guarantee** - 30 days refund policy
- **Case studies** - Show results from current users
- **Social proof** - Testimonials and reviews
