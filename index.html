Fitness Tips

Calorie Burning Tips

HIIT workouts can burn up to 25-30% more calories than traditional cardio. Consider adding interval training to boost your burn.

Optimal Workout Times

Morning workouts (6-8 AM) show 20% better consistency rates. Evening workouts (5-7 PM) may yield slightly higher performance.

Nutrition Tips

Consume protein within 30 minutes post-workout for optimal muscle recovery. Stay hydrated - aim for 8 glasses daily.

Recovery Tips

Get 7-9 hours of sleep for optimal muscle recovery. Take rest days - your body needs them to grow stronger.
